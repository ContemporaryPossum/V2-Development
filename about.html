Who we are

A Selangor developer dedicated to high-quality, value-added homes, and to the creation of innovative industrial buildings.

Established in 2019, V2 Development Sdn. Bhd. handles the development, construction and management of every project it delivers, keeping quality in the hands of one accountable team from first plan to final handover.

We build communities with complementary architecture and facilities, using a mix of house types scaled to their surroundings, so that each new neighbourhood feels considered, connected and lasting.
