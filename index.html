We build homes the way we would want our own families to live in them. Every development is a chance to lift the look and life of the neighbourhood around it: safe streets, practical layouts, freehold land and individual titles, at prices working families can reach.

V2 Development Sdn. Bhd. was incorporated in Selangor in 2019. We develop residential and industrial property, acquire land, and build in joint venture with landowners.

    Own land in Selangor? Let's build on it together.

      We buy residential land outright and take it through approvals, construction and handover.
